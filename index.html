Our mission

No child should feel alone in a hospital hallway.

A hospital stay can be long, scary, and lonely, especially for a child. Our mission is to uplift the spirits of children stuck in hospitals by bringing comfort, joy, and hope to pediatric patients.

We do that through hands-on projects like toy drives and care packages, and by giving students and community members meaningful ways to make a difference.

This is only the beginning, and we are so excited to grow Hope in the Halls into a community dedicated to giving comfort, joy, and hope to pediatric patients.

What we do

Our projects

Toy Drives

We collect new toys, games, books, and activities and deliver them to pediatric patients, bringing a little fun and normalcy to long hospital days.

Care Packages

Each package is filled with comfort items and a handwritten note, so kids and their families know a whole community is cheering them on.

More on the way

As we grow, we'll launch new projects to support young patients. Have an idea? We'd love to hear it.
