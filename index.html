Our mission

Small moments of hope, repeated every day.

We believe that hope grows in ordinary places: between classes, outside a hospital room, in the hallway where someone needs a kind word. Our mission is to make sure those moments of care happen on purpose, not by chance.

Hope in the Halls was founded by people who saw a need in their own community and decided to meet it. Today we partner with local volunteers, families, and organizations to reach more people each year.

What we do

Our programs

Encouragement Notes

Volunteers write and deliver handwritten notes of encouragement, reminding people that someone is in their corner.

Peer Support

Trained mentors offer a listening ear and connect people to resources when they need more help.

Care Kits

We assemble and distribute kits with everyday essentials and comfort items for those going through a tough season.
