Welcome to the Hope in the Halls blog!

Hope in the Halls exists to uplift the spirits of children stuck in hospitals. Through projects like toy drives and care packages, we bring comfort, joy, and hope to pediatric patients. Right now, the toys and care packages we collect are donated to pediatric patients at Kaiser Permanente Roseville.

As we grow, we want to give students and community members meaningful ways to get involved: volunteering, writing for this blog, and stepping into leadership positions.

This is only the beginning, and we are so excited to turn Hope in the Halls into a community dedicated to giving comfort, joy, and hope to pediatric patients.
